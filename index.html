Remember numbers with Pokemon

Break a number into pairs, then turn each pair into a Pokemon or a move, alternating between the two. Link each image to the next to build a scene you can recall.

Weekday quick guide

The card group is the year code: for card NN it is the weekday of March 4 in 19NN. Add the month code and the day of the month, then take the total modulo 7: 0 = Sunday, 6 = Saturday. January and February use the leap-year value in leap years. For 2000, add the century correction +6. Example: July 4, 1976: card 76 group 4 + July 6 + 4 = 14 → Sunday. December 31, 1999: card 99 group 4 + December 5 + 31 = 40 → Friday.
  1. Find the card for year 19NN and note its weekday group.
    • Sunday

    The 0–9 cards are single-digit entries; the colors apply to the 00–99 year cards.

    For 2000, add 6 to the 00 year code. 1900-03-04 to 2000-03-04 is 100 years plus 25 leap days = 36,525 days. 36,525 mod 7 = 6, so 2000's year code is 6 (Saturday) instead of 1900's 0 (Sunday).

  2. Add the month code and the day of the month.

    Code 0/6 3/2 3 6 1 4 6 2 5 0 3 5
  3. Take the total modulo 7. 0 = Sunday … 6 = Saturday.
